Who We Are

Zackconnect Autos is a Ghanaian vehicle import service. We help customers source cars from China, the USA, and Canada, and offer Bajaj tricycles in Ghana. Cars shown as import options are not represented as stock physically in Ghana.

Our goal is to make vehicle ownership easier by providing a reliable source for quality vehicles and professional import services.

Our Mission

To provide a trusted, customer-focused vehicle dealership that simplifies car and tricycle buying, and offers transparent import services in Ghana.

Our Vision

To become Ghana's leading vehicle dealership, known for reliability, transparency, quality vehicles, and excellent customer service.

Our Values

  • Trust and transparency in all dealings
  • Quality vehicles at fair prices
  • Customer-focused service
  • Professional expertise
  • Clear and honest communication

What We Offer

Car Imports and Bajaj Tricycles

We help customers source cars from China, the USA, and Canada for import to Ghana, and offer Bajaj tricycles for personal and business transportation needs. Import listings are subject to confirmation and do not represent stock already in Ghana.

Vehicle Import Services

We help customers source and import vehicles from China, Canada, and the USA with transparent pricing and expert guidance.

Customer Service Commitment

Our team is dedicated to helping you find the right vehicle and providing support throughout your purchase journey.
