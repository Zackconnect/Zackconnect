VEHICLE SOURCING & IMPORTS

How Our Import Service Works

The Import Process

  1. The customer submits the vehicle requirements
  2. Zackconnect Autos reviews the request
  3. The company checks sourcing options
  4. Zackconnect Autos prepares a quotation based on the vehicle and applicable costs
  5. The customer reviews and approves the agreed terms
  6. Import arrangements proceed according to the agreement

Cost Breakdown

The final quotation may include the following items:

  • Vehicle purchase price
  • Shipping costs
  • Insurance
  • Applicable duties and taxes
  • Port charges
  • Clearing fees
  • Local delivery (where relevant)

Note: Import charges vary based on vehicle type, origin, and current regulations. Availability, costs, and timelines depend on the specific vehicle, supplier, shipping arrangements, and applicable requirements. We provide transparent quotations with no hidden fees.
